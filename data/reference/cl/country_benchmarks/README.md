# Chile country-level exposome benchmarks

`pm25_2017.csv` records the official national indicator used in the ecological
assignment demonstration. It was transcribed from page 125 of the Chilean
Ministry of the Environment's *Cuarto Reporte del Estado del Medio Ambiente*.

The reported 2017 value is 26 µg/m³. It is population-weighted across 29
monitoring stations with population representativity. It is therefore a
country-level indicator for monitored cities, not a wall-to-wall mean over the
entire territory and not an individual exposure measurement.

Source: https://sinia.mma.gob.cl/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/copy-10-calidad-del-aire-pdf.pdf
